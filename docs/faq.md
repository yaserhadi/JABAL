# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is Jabal?
Jabal is a multi-tenant application that helps organizations manage their operations with role-based access control and comprehensive audit tracking.

### How do I get started?
Check out our [Getting Started Guide](index.md) for an overview, then visit the specific guide for your role in the [guides](guides/) section.

### Who do I contact for help?
Contact your system administrator or refer to the guides for your specific role.

## Account & Access

### How do I log in?
Your administrator will provide you with login credentials. Use these credentials at the login page to access the system.

### I forgot my password, what should I do?
Contact your system administrator to reset your password.

### What roles are available?
The system supports multiple roles including Admin, Manager, and User. Each role has different permissions and capabilities. See the relevant guide for your role for more details.

## Troubleshooting

### The page isn't loading. What should I do?
1. Check your internet connection
2. Try refreshing the page
3. Clear your browser cache
4. If the problem persists, contact your system administrator

### I'm seeing an error message. What does it mean?
Error messages are designed to be self-explanatory. If you're unsure what an error means, note the exact message and contact your system administrator.

## More Help

If your question isn't answered here, check the [guides](guides/) section or contact your administrator.
